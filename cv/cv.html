Research Interests

I work on localization for field robots. My focus is LiDAR-inertial odometry that stays accurate under fast motion and vibration, and global localization that bounds drift over large outdoor missions without loop closure or GNSS. I build these methods into complete systems and test them on legged and wheeled robots in challenges and industry projects.
